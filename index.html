How it works

A full 60-episode series is delivered in 7–14 days per language, in batches of 15 episodes. We can run up to 3 series in parallel.

1 — You send

Episode masters, the music & effects (M&E) stem, and the script or English subtitles. You warrant the rights to the material; all work is work-for-hire.

2 — We localize

Adapted translation, voice casting locked per character for the entire series, dubbing, mix, and two-layer QC: technical checks plus a native-speaker pass.

3 — You review

One revision round per batch is included (two on Premium Hybrid). Rights transfer in full on final payment.

  • Terms: 50% deposit to start, balance per delivered batch. PayPal, Wise or bank transfer.
  • Rush delivery (48 hours): +50%.

FAQ

What do you need from us to start?

Three things: the episode master videos, the music & effects (M&E) stem for each episode, and the script or English subtitles. The M&E stem matters — it lets us replace voices while keeping your music and sound design intact.

Who owns the rights to the localized version?

You do. All work is work-for-hire and transfers to you in full on final payment. We only work on material you own or are licensed to localize, and we ask you to warrant that when you send it.

How fast can you deliver a full series?

A 60-episode series takes 7–14 days per language, delivered in batches of 15 so you can start releasing before the series is complete. A watermarked test episode of your first title is ready within 2 working days.

What if we don't like the voices?

Casting is locked per character only after you approve the test episode — that's exactly what it's for. Every batch also includes one revision round (two on Premium Hybrid), so feedback is applied before the series continues.

How do we pay?

50% deposit to start, balance per delivered batch, by PayPal, Wise or bank transfer. Series of 60+ episodes of a single title get 15% off automatically.
